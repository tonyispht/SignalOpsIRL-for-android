<p>Signal OPS IRL listens to nearby Bluetooth Low Energy and Classic Bluetooth broadcasts and turns them into a retro "field intel" terminal, with XP, levels, achievements, daily quests and a device collection to fill. It's also a hands-on way to learn how Bluetooth works and to check what your own devices are broadcasting.</p>

<p><b>Please use it responsibly.</b> Signal OPS IRL is for recreational, educational and personal-security use. Every user accepts the Terms of Responsible Use before the app will scan.</p>

<p><b>Scanning</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Continuous or battery-saving burst scanning, BLE 5 extended advertising and Classic Bluetooth discovery</li>
<li>Live signal list with signal strength, proximity colours and a radar view</li>
<li>External USB Bluetooth adapter support for better range</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Identifying devices, fully offline</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Built-in Bluetooth database: every Bluetooth SIG company ID, service UUID and device type, plus the IEEE manufacturer list</li>
<li>Recognises phones, earbuds, watches, trackers, sensors, smart-home gear, drones broadcasting Remote ID and much more</li>
<li>Exact model names for hundreds of earbuds and speakers (Fast Pair, AirPods/Beats, Galaxy Buds)</li>
<li>Decodes sensor readings (Ruuvi, Xiaomi, Govee, SwitchBot) and raw advertising data</li>
<li>Export the devices it could not identify, with personal names and full addresses removed, to help improve the rules</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Personal security</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Tracker-follow detection: warns when an AirTag, Tile, SmartTag or Find Hub tag keeps showing up around you</li>
<li>Flags BLE "spam" tools that flood phones with fake pairing pop-ups</li>
<li>Watchlist alerts, hot/cold locating for lost devices, and a pairing check for your own gear</li>
</ul>

<p><b>The game</b></p>
<ul>
<li>XP and levels, 20 achievements, unlockable titles, daily quests, streaks and a login calendar</li>
<li>The Signal Dex: a collection of device types to fill</li>
<li>Optional movie-style effects: boot sequence, CRT power on/off, scanlines, data rain</li>
</ul>

<p>No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Free and open source (MIT).</p>
